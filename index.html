About us

A family business, two generations of Master Electricians

Founded and operated by Simon Beckett in 1999, Nomis is dedicated to superior workmanship and prompt, efficient service. Joined by his daughter Monique, Nomis engages the next generation, bringing in a new era of vision and innovation.

President and CEO

Simon Beckett

A fully certified Master Electrician with experience in all aspects of the electrical field. Simon supports the Ontario Youth Apprenticeship Program (OYAP) and co-op education, has mentored many apprentices, and is a fully qualified floor heat and repair technician.

Master Electrician

Monique Beckett

Monique started her apprenticeship in high school and became fully licensed in 2001 at 21. Now a fully certified Master Electrician, she specializes in high-end fixture installations, is trained in home automation, has a great eye for lighting design, and oversees most residential projects.
